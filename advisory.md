# Advisory

Alongside full-time work, I take on a small number of short, fixed-scope advisory engagements. Each one draws on what I've run at enterprise scale.

| Engagement | What you get | Typical length |
|---|---|---|
| **Vulnerability management health check** | A review of your current programme, an exploit-driven prioritisation model ([CS01](case-studies/CS01-Exploit-Driven-Vulnerability-Prioritization/README.md)), SLA and KPI design, and a 90-day roadmap | 2 to 3 weeks |
| **Security tool evaluation (Go / No-Go)** | Evaluation criteria, a pilot plan, scoring and a recommendation pack your leadership can sign off | 3 to 4 weeks |
| **Security tool rollout playbook** | Rollout batches, a security champions model, a KPI dashboard spec, and communication and support plans for GHAS, Snyk, Qualys, Defender and similar tools ([CS35](case-studies/CS35-GitHub-Advanced-Security-Enterprise-Rollout/README.md)) | 2 to 4 weeks |
| **Compliance readiness sprint** | A gap assessment and prioritised plan for DPDP, PCI DSS 4.0.1 or EU CRA ([CS34](case-studies/CS34-FS-DPDP-Act-Readiness-Automation/README.md)) | 2 to 3 weeks |

## How I work

- **Start small and fixed-scope.** A short engagement with a clear deliverable, so you see value before committing to more.
- **Measure what matters.** Coverage, time to fix and SLA performance, not the number of findings closed.
- **Leave it running.** Playbooks, dashboards and owners your team can keep using after I've gone.

A 30-minute call is the easiest start: tell me what's happening, and I'll suggest where to begin. Email pankhuri.awasthi@gmail.com.
