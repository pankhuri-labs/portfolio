# Insourcing a service from vendors, a month and a half early

**Role:** Associate Project Manager, Content Shared Services (Aug 2018 to May 2020) · **Organisation:** John Deere

## The situation

Content Shared Services for two regions were run by outsourced vendors, and the decision had been made to bring them in-house. The transition contract was signed, but its execution scope was thin, and the business stakeholders, comfortable with the vendor, had never really agreed to the change.

## What I did

- Worked out the skills the work needed and built the team from scratch: **13 hires**, deliberately mixing in-house and vendor skill sets, growing to 16 people within a year.
- Went to the people who had finalised the contract, raised the gaps, and got their support for a concrete plan.
- Set clear ownership and expectations for each person and planned early hands-on work, so the team learned on the real work while the vendor was still there.
- Kept marketing timelines and translation quality steady, and at the same time rolled out national dealer websites in **10+ countries in seven months**.

## The result

- The transition was planned for 15 August to 15 November. The team was confidently running the work by early to mid October, **a month and a half early**.
- The second region's transition was reduced to handing over access and contacts.
- About 80% of hires stayed a year or more, and every in-house team member was promoted by the time I moved on.

## What I learned

We under-planned the handoffs that translation adds to content publishing. For the next batches I built in extra time and gave translation coordinators and country contacts advance notice of releases.
