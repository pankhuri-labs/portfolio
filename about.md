# About me

I've spent 20 years where technology meets the organisation that has to adopt it: 12 in engineering and IBM consulting, then eight at John Deere as the client, in project, product and security leadership.

**Application security and vulnerability management (Dec 2024 to Sep 2026).** As AGM and Technology Product Manager for Application Security and Vulnerability Management at John Deere, I led the security platforms used by thousands of developers, working with AppSec and VM teams across India and the US.
- Led the evaluation and rollout of an enterprise code-scanning platform in four months, replacing the incumbent SAST tool and a home-grown secret scanner. This cut annual licence cost substantially, blocked hundreds of secrets with push protection, roughly doubled AppSec findings fixed within SLA, and the rollout team won the CISO Award.
- Delivered with a new 12-person AppSec engineering team built by my engineering manager, and grew a 100-person security champions network.
- Led the evaluation, pilot and rollout of an endpoint protection platform, and supported Engineering's container scanning rollout with communication and stakeholder alignment.
- Ran risk-based vulnerability management on an enterprise VM platform, with remediation SLAs and KPI reporting for leadership.

**Product management (May 2020 to Nov 2024).** As Technology Product Manager for Parts Information, I owned the roadmap for two Scrum teams serving 500 users worldwide, with about 24 releases a year. I replaced 20+ years of legacy systems with decoupled components, made content delivery 10x faster, saved 4,500 hours a year with a parts lookup utility, and saved $114K a year by bringing repeated content authoring in-house.

**Building and leading teams (Aug 2018 to May 2020).** As Associate Project Manager for Content Shared Services, I built a team from scratch (13 hires) to take content services for two regions back in-house from vendors, a month and a half ahead of plan, and rolled out national dealer websites in 10+ countries in seven months.

**Engineering and IBM consulting (2004 to 2018).** Java/J2EE and Documentum development at HCL, Infosys and IBM, then IBM technical lead for 16 developers across India, the US and Mexico on Deere.com, live in 40+ countries and 20+ languages.
