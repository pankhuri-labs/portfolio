# About me

I've spent 20 years where technology meets the organisation that has to adopt it: 12 in engineering and IBM consulting, then eight at John Deere as the client, in project, product and security leadership.

**Application security and vulnerability management (Dec 2024 to Sep 2026).** As AGM and Technology Product Manager for Application Security and Vulnerability Management at John Deere, I led the security platforms used by 10,000+ developers, with 22 people across AppSec and VM.
- Led the evaluation and rollout of GitHub Advanced Security to 55,000+ repositories in four months, replacing the incumbent SAST tool and a home-grown secret scanner. This removed $990K a year in licence cost, blocked ~600 secrets with push protection, lifted AppSec findings fixed within SLA from 35% to ~70%, and won the CISO Award.
- Built the 12-person AppSec team from the ground up and a 100-person security champions network.
- Led the evaluation, pilot and rollout of Microsoft Defender for Endpoint, reaching 96% coverage, and supported Engineering's CrowdStrike container scanning rollout with communication and stakeholder alignment.
- On Qualys, identified why late-found findings were missing their SLAs, directed the investigation and owned the stakeholder communication while the risk ratings were fixed.

**Product management (May 2020 to Nov 2024).** As Technology Product Manager for Parts Information, I owned the roadmap for two Scrum teams serving 500 users worldwide, with about 24 releases a year. I replaced 20+ years of legacy systems with decoupled components, made content delivery 10x faster, saved 4,500 hours a year with the Express Utility, and saved $114K a year by bringing repeated content authoring in-house from a parallel RPA tool.

**Building and leading teams (Aug 2018 to May 2020).** As Associate Project Manager for Content Shared Services, I built a team from scratch (13 hires) to take content services for two regions back in-house from vendors, a month and a half ahead of plan, and rolled out national dealer websites in 10+ countries in seven months.

**Engineering and IBM consulting (2004 to 2018).** Java/J2EE and Documentum development at HCL, Infosys and IBM, then IBM technical lead for 16 developers across India, the US and Mexico on Deere.com, live in 40+ countries and 20+ languages.
