# About me

I've spent more than 20 years where technology meets the organisation that has to adopt it.

**Application security and vulnerability management (2024 to 2026).** As Technology Product Manager at John Deere, I owned the Qualys vulnerability management platform and enterprise-wide adoption of Microsoft Defender for Endpoint, and led the evaluation and rollout of GitHub Advanced Security and CrowdStrike container scanning. I managed two cross-functional teams and translated technical risk into business terms for leadership.

**Product ownership (2021 to 2024).** I ran two Scrum teams in parts information engineering, replaced 20+ years of legacy systems with modern components, and delivered features that saved thousands of hours a year.

**Global web platforms (2011 to 2021).** First as an ECM consultant with IBM, then as an associate project manager, I helped build and run Deere.com across 40+ countries and led content shared services and dealer website rollouts.

**Engineering roots (2004 to 2011).** Documentum, TeamSite and Java/J2EE development at HCL, IBM and Infosys, including e-commerce work for European retail clients.

## How I work

- **Start small and fixed-scope.** A short engagement with a clear deliverable, so you see value before committing to more.
- **Measure what matters.** Coverage, time to fix and SLA performance, not the number of findings closed.
- **Leave it running.** Playbooks, dashboards and owners your team can keep using after I've gone.
